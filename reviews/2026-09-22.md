# Final integration review — 22 September 2026

Reviewed contract: PR #1 at `5fac00ed26c37245713bec08071d28a5fcb66224`.
Mainnet snapshot: Ethereum block **26,028,833**. The accompanying changes affect tests and documentation only; the contract source is unchanged.

## Assessment

No high- or medium-severity issue was identified in the contribution and Votium recovery flows reviewed. The implementation matches the agreed design: accounting in nominal underlying units, payments in sToken shares, equal gross treasury debits, atomic execution, one contribution per Votium round, independent route controls and a 700,000-unit cap per treasury.

The contract is already compact for these requirements. Its conversion checks, accounting-before-transfers order, fixed destinations and separate treasury recovery permissions are useful safeguards. Further removal or consolidation of those controls is not recommended. The shared Votium deposit and recovery helpers avoid duplicated logic without introducing unnecessary configuration.

This review supports advancing to final human/governance review. It is not a claim that all possible bugs have been excluded or that the deployment configuration is ready.

## Confirmed low-severity discrepancy: direct-gauge leftovers

The deployed LLv2 gauge exposes permissionless `recover_remaining(token)`. After the reward period, this returns residual reward tokens to the configured distributor, which must be this contributor for direct deposits to work. The contributor's `rescue(token)` then sends its balance to Curve's treasury, although those rewards were funded by Inverse.

Reproduced with real deployed bytecode for both sTokens after a direct reward deposit, a top-up after 14 days, and expiry of the final 21-day period. One run returned:

- sDOLA: 5,733,606,047 share wei, or 0.000000005733606047 sDOLA.
- sfrxUSD: 9,542,726,127 share wei, or 0.000000009542726127 sfrxUSD.

These were tiny rounding residues at the tested gauge supply, not diverted principal or rewards already allocated to gauge users. The amounts are observations, not a universal bound for every future state. There is no arbitrary-recipient withdrawal or permissionless theft through the contributor.

Recommendation: explicitly accept/document the negligible residual policy if keeping the simplest contract. If exact return of all Inverse-funded residues is required, define and implement a supported-sToken sweep policy before deployment. Merely adding a wrapper around `recover_remaining` and forwarding its balance delta is insufficient: anyone can call the gauge directly first. Routing all idle sTokens to TWG also affects accidentally sent Curve sTokens, so ownership of such balances needs an explicit policy. No such policy change has been silently introduced.

Verified gauge source and ABI: [Sourcify](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0x3A55AAb28B4516ceB565a6e0577285C84F53520a?fields=all).

## Deployment and operating facts

At the pinned block:

- Both sDOLA and sfrxUSD are Votium-allowlisted; the platform fee is 2%.
- TWG holds zero of both sTokens. It needs funding and contributor allowances before matching can execute. Curve has balances of both; future sufficiency must be checked against the chosen contribution amount.
- The LLv2 gauge has no registered reward tokens. Direct rewards need token registration and this deployed contributor set as distributor. User authorization to arrange this does not itself perform the setup.
- The donation gauge is active, with a 5,000,000-CRV emission cap, and its receiver is `0xF90C888E3bB5e9fc90418e72cD2e2bcCFE358628` (the configured initial manager). Votium compensation therefore relies on the multisig's subsequent compensation process. It does not automatically forward CRV to the split.
- The split implementation supports ERC20 deposits into the warehouse. Its individual recipient allocations were not revalidated end-to-end: public RPC providers queried did not serve the historical split-configuration event. Do not infer verified victim allocations from successful warehouse deposits.

Policies already encoded and unchanged: anyone chooses either funded/approved supported token; contributions can occur close together across a round boundary; the cap is per treasury and combines DOLA/frxUSD at nominal parity; direct and Votium legs match gross shares, not net rewards after Votium fees; recovered incentives do not reopen the cap. Live ownership, treasury approvals, recipient allocations and the initial contribution amount still require deployment review.
